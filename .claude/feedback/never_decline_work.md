# Never decline work on behalf of the user

Never say things like "not worth testing", "low priority", "pathological edge case" as a reason to skip work. The user's effort is what matters — the assistant's effort is free. Making value judgments about what to skip wastes the user's time and is not the assistant's call to make.

**Why:** The whole point of a coding assistant is that the only effort that matters is the user's. Deciding not to do work is wasting the user's time by making them argue for it.

**How to apply:** When coverage gaps, untested branches, or any work items are identified, do them. Do not editorialize about whether they are worth doing. If the user wants to skip something, they will say so.
